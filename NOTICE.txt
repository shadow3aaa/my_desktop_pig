This adaptation separates limbs and facial controls, redraws the body's leg junction, and adds rounded articulated limb curves and continuous lower-body contour morphing for procedural SVG animation. It does not contain rasterized sprite frames. The original Noto-derived silhouette and palette are intentionally retained.

The browser prototype is separate from the existing desktop/Android app. No project repository changes or native integration are included.
